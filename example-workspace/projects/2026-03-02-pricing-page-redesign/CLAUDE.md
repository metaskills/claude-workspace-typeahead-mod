# Pricing Page Redesign - Mar 2026

## Goal

Replace the single-plan pricing page with three tiers (Starter, Team, Enterprise) and an annual billing toggle that shows the discount.

## Status

- [x] Competitor teardown of five pricing pages
- [x] Tier names and limits agreed with sales
- [ ] Copy for the comparison table
- [ ] A/B test plan against the current page

## Findings

- Four of the five competitors lead with the middle tier. We will too.
- The annual toggle lifts annual sign-ups when the saving is shown as a dollar amount, not a percent.

Finish the comparison table copy, then hand off to design for the Figma pass.
